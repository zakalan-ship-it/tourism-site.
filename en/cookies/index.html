Cookie Policy

November 13, 2026

This website uses a small amount of browser storage to work properly. We only use optional cookies if you agree to them.

Necessary storage

  • Language preference (wx-lang) — remembers the language you chose.
  • Cookie consent (wx-consent) — remembers your choices in the cookie banner.

Analytics and marketing

No analytics or advertising cookies are currently set. If we add them in future, they will only be activated after you consent in the cookie banner.

Third-party content

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Links to WhatsApp, maps or social networks open those services, which apply their own privacy policies.

Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer, or delete stored data in your browser settings.
