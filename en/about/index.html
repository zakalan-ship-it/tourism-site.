Who we are

About WellXplore

We are an Istanbul-based incoming tour operator. Our team plans and runs guided tours, private experiences and transfers across Türkiye for travellers from all over the world — and for the travel agencies who send them.

WellXplore

Built in Istanbul, made for the world

WellXplore was founded by travel professionals who grew up between Europe and Asia — on the shores of the Bosphorus. We believe a great journey comes from local knowledge, honest advice and attention to the small details that make a trip feel effortless.

Our name says what we stand for: exploring well. That means carefully designed routes without exhausting drives, hotels chosen for location and comfort, licensed guides who love their country, and support that is there when you need it.

We welcome independent travellers, families, groups and partner agencies from Europe, Asia and beyond — with service in English, Chinese, German and Turkish.

Local expertise

Every itinerary is designed and operated by our own team in Türkiye.

Honest information

Clear inclusions, realistic schedules and no hidden extras.

Care on the ground

A real person to call, 24 hours a day, throughout your trip.

Responsible travel

We work with local guides, family-run restaurants and small hotels wherever we can.

Company

What we do

  • Scheduled small-group tours across Türkiye
  • Private and tailor-made itineraries
  • Day tours and experiences in Istanbul, Cappadocia and on the coast
  • Airport, city and intercity transfers
  • Groups, incentives and MICE ground handling
  • Services for travel agencies and tour operators (B2B)

For travel agencies

We are a reliable ground partner for agencies worldwide: series departures, FIT services, guides in many languages, transport and hotels at net rates.
