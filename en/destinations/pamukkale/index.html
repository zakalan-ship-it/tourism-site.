Pamukkale

The “cotton castle” of white travertine terraces

Destination

The “cotton castle” of white travertine terraces

Warm, mineral-rich springs have covered this hillside in brilliant white terraces filled with turquoise pools. Above them lie the ruins of Hierapolis, a Roman spa city with a vast necropolis and a theatre.

Best time to visit
Spring and autumn. Visit early or late in the day for softer light and fewer visitors.
Getting there
Denizli Çardak Airport (DNZ) is 1 hour away; Pamukkale is also a natural stop between Cappadocia and Ephesus.
