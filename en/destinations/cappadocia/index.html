Cappadocia

Fairy chimneys, cave hotels and balloon-filled skies

Destination

Fairy chimneys, cave hotels and balloon-filled skies

Volcanic eruptions and millions of years of wind shaped Cappadocia into valleys of cones, pillars and rock churches. People have carved homes, monasteries and whole underground cities into the soft stone.

Best time to visit
April–June and September–October. Winter brings snow-covered valleys; balloon flights operate all year, weather permitting.
Getting there
About 1 hour 20 minutes by air from Istanbul to Kayseri (ASR) or Nevşehir (NAV) airport, then a 1-hour transfer.
