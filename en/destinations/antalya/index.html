Antalya

Old harbour, waterfalls and the Turquoise Coast

Destination

Old harbour, waterfalls and the Turquoise Coast

Antalya combines a Roman-walled old town with long beaches, mountain backdrops and waterfalls that fall straight into the sea. It is the gateway to ancient Perge and Aspendos and to the rafting canyons of the Taurus Mountains.

Best time to visit
May–October for beaches; spring and autumn are ideal for sightseeing and hiking.
Getting there
Antalya Airport (AYT) has direct flights from across Europe and a 1-hour flight from Istanbul.
