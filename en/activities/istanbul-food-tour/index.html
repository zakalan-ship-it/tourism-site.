Two Continents Food Tour

Taste 12+ specialities from Karaköy to Kadıköy, with a ferry in between.

4 hours Istanbul

About this experience

Start with a Turkish breakfast and baklava in Karaköy, take the ferry to the Asian side and graze through Kadıköy market: meze, pide, kebab, pickles, Turkish coffee and more. Come hungry.
